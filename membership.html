Find your place in SIG.

Whether your interest is research, practical skills, awareness, communications or mentorship, there is a department to explore.

Who should join?

SIG is primarily for medical students at AlFaisal University who are interested in surgery and want to learn, contribute and connect.

Ways to participate

  • Join department activities.
  • Participate in research opportunities.
  • Attend workshops and awareness events.
  • Present at journal clubs.
  • Engage with mentorship opportunities.
  • Represent SIG at appropriate external conferences and events.
The official membership application process and eligibility rules will be added here once finalized.
