About SIG

A student-led community for future surgeons.

Surgery Interest Group at AlFaisal University gives medical students opportunities to explore surgery through research, training, awareness, mentorship and engagement with the wider surgical community.

Who we are

SIG is a student organization at AlFaisal University, College of Medicine. We aim to create meaningful opportunities for medical students who are curious about surgery — whether their interest begins with a journal club, a workshop, a research project, a mentor or a conference.

Our ambition

Our long-term ambition is to grow into a recognized student surgical organization in Saudi Arabia and beyond, with international visibility, conference participation, strong research opportunities and high-quality educational programming.

Who can engage?

Our core community is medical students. Our wider network can include residents, fellows, physicians, surgeons and others interested in surgery. Members of the public may also engage with appropriate awareness events and open activities.
